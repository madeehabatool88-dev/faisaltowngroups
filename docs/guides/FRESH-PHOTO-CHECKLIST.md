# Fresh site photography checklist

Use original photographs of the actual location. Keep archived January 2026 material clearly labeled until replacement images are verified. Renderings and posters can remain only with their promotional labels.

## First batch: 10–12 useful photographs

| Priority | Photographs | Where they will be used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Phase 2 entrance/signage and the actual approach road: 2 photos | Homepage development section and location guide |
| 2 | Sector T: one wide street view and one real 5.56 marla plot example, only if currently available | Featured offer and Sector T page |
| 3 | O, Q and R: one recognizable street/layout view in each sector | Distinguish the three sector guides |
| 4 | Sector P and Sector S: one current street/development view each | Their respective guides |
| 5 | K Block: 1–2 views of a genuine 10 marla-or-larger option, only if one is currently offered | Availability enquiry guidance; do not present installment terms without confirmation |
| 6 | Optional real office or representative photo, if you want it published | Business identity and contact area |

If no exact plot is available or cleared for publication, send a sector streetscape and label it as a location reference. A photograph does not establish ownership, approvals, possession, utilities or current inventory.

## Information to send with each image

- Original file and actual date taken.
- Phase, sector/block, street and nearest identifiable landmark.
- Location pin if available; indicate whether it may be published.
- A short factual description of what the image shows.
- For a plot example: size, plot reference if public, availability confirmation date, and whether any quoted terms are confirmed. Default to full-payment wording. No 5/5.56 marla installment offer; K Block 10 marla and above remains conditional.
- Confirmation that you own the image or have permission to publish it.

Suggested filename: `phase2-sector-t-street-2026-10-28-01.jpg` (use the real capture date).

## How to shoot and send

- Prefer landscape 4:3 or 3:2 images, at least 2000 pixels wide, with a level horizon and daylight.
- Send original JPG/HEIC files as documents or in a ZIP, rather than screenshots or WhatsApp-compressed photos. Website derivatives will be generated separately.
- Take a wide establishing shot plus a closer shot showing useful detail. Avoid filters, decorative text, exaggerated perspective or promotional overlays.
- Include the street or sector sign where possible. Do not conceal unfinished roads or work in progress.
- Avoid publishing identifiable visitors, vehicle plates, private documents or contact details without permission.
- Optional: a steady 15–30 second walkthrough of the same location, with its capture date and location supplied separately.

## Genuine business information still needed

Business/representative name, office or meeting location (if one exists), Google Maps link, opening hours, and any verifiable dealer/partner credentials you want published. Leave non-applicable fields empty. WhatsApp can remain the only enquiry channel.

## Publishing checks

Match each image to its location, write a factual caption and alt text, show the capture date, and remove embedded GPS metadata from the public copy when the location should not be disclosed. Keep original files privately for verification. Reconfirm stock before describing any image as an available plot.
